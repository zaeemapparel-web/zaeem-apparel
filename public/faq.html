COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQs

Everything you need to know about Zaeem Apparel.

How long does delivery take? +
Standard delivery takes 3-5 working days across Pakistan. Major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad) usually receive orders within 2-3 days.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery? +
Yes! Cash on Delivery is available all over Pakistan. You can pay when you receive your order.
What is your return policy? +
We offer a 7-day return and exchange policy. Products must be unused and in their original packaging. See our Return Policy for full details.
How do I choose the right size? +
Please refer to our Size Guide. All measurements are in inches. If you're between sizes, we recommend choosing the larger size.
How can I track my order? +
Once your order ships, we'll send you the tracking number via WhatsApp or SMS. You can also contact us directly on WhatsApp for updates.
Is my payment information safe? +
Absolutely. We never store any payment information. For Cash on Delivery orders, you pay directly to the courier.
What if I receive a damaged product? +
Contact us immediately on WhatsApp with photos. We'll replace damaged items at no cost to you within 7 days of delivery.
Do you ship internationally? +
Currently we ship within Pakistan only. International shipping will be available soon.
