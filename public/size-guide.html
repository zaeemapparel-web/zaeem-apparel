GUIDE

How to Measure

01

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal and comfortably loose.

02

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline — the narrowest part of your torso.

03

Length

Measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the desired hemline.

04

Shoulder

Measure from the edge of one shoulder, across your back, to the other shoulder.

05

Sleeve

Measure from the shoulder seam down to the end of the sleeve, keeping your arm slightly bent.
